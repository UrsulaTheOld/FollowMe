Gives the camera tracking capabilities. 

Written for release 1.5. 
UrsulaTheOld
Added Colonists to the original choices - requested by MortalSmurph for his 24/7 run. The Colonist choice will follow colonists, colony mech, and colony ghouls. It will randomly follow an animal. The pawns are weighted with an "interest" factor. Pawns who are involved in combat have the highest interest factor, followed by those putting out fires, and lastly doctoring. Sleeping colonists and animals have the lowest interest weight.

While running the mod during MortalSmurph's 24/7 run, the camera stopped during the first of three overnight runs. The camera will stop if the followed pawn goes off the map or is destroyed. This can be reproduced using dev mod. Under normal play (not afk overnight play) this will rarely happen. I made the decision to not modify Karel Kroeze's code since it happens infrequently.

Please see Karel's links below for the rest of the features of the mod. Thank you Karel and everyone else who worked on this mod!

![Features](https://headers.karel-kroeze.nl/title/Features.png)  
Press the 'Home' key (default, can be changed with in-game keybinding settings) while any colonist, enemy, animal or item is selected, and the camera will follow them (including furniture and plants - but they tend to not move much). Alternatively, you can click with the middle mouse button on colonists in the Colonist Bar on top of the screen to toggle following.

**Does not require a new save game**
Can safely be enabled/disabled in ongoing saves.
